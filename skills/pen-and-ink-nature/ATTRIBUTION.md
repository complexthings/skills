# Attribution

The pen-and-ink style this skill describes was studied from **pendrawings.me**, written and
drawn by **Rahul Jain**.

- Site: <https://pendrawings.me>

Rahul Jain's tutorials are the reason this skill knows that tone is built by parallel-line
count rather than pen pressure, that the fine-texture mark is dots *and* ticks rather than pure
stipple, that a narrow form takes two tones with a reserved white centre, and that irregularity
is an instruction rather than an accident. That reading is the whole basis of the guidance here.

## What is and is not redistributed

**No source material ships in this skill.** Not one image, not one sentence, not one file from
pendrawings.me.

- **The prose is original.** Every technique and subject file was written fresh from what the
  tutorials teach. Nothing is quoted, paraphrased line by line, or reproduced.
- **The exemplar images are generated.** The contact sheets under `resources/techniques/` are
  new images produced for this skill. No pendrawings.me pixel appears in them.
- **The taxonomy is ours.** The split into seven techniques and six subject families is this
  skill's organisation, not the source's. The source teaches subject by subject and introduces
  marks wherever a subject needs them.

The tutorials are copyrighted by their author. They were read as study material only. If you
want the original instruction — the step photographs, the worked examples, the printable
templates — go to the site.
